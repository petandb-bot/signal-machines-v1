Four little machines

Listen, compare, then patch them together.

These WAVs are actual offline DSP renders. Human listening quality has not been evaluated. Comparison tracks use one constant gain toward a common RMS, capped at 4× and 0.9 peak. This preserves each track's dynamics. Raw measurements and gains are in the render report.

The same eight-second recording contains a metal strike, harmonic sustain, irregular noise pulses, quiet synthetic room sound, then silence. CUTS and FOUND use that recording as their sample. NERVE takes its rectified energy plus a common 137 ms trigger train; NERVE/CHANCE/UNSPENT events strike the same short test tone so you can hear their timing.
